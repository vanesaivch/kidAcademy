Home Game

Welcome, little explorer!

Hop over to our game room and let's play!

How to play

Flip a card – Choose two cards and turn them over.

If they match, you keep them! If not, turn them back over.

Keep going until you've matched all the cards!

Remember! – Pay attention to where each card is.

A Note for Parents 💛

Dear Parents,

Play is one of the most natural and meaningful ways children learn. Every game they play offers an opportunity to discover, practise new skills, and build confidence. Our Matching Card Game is designed to make learning fun while helping children develop important skills such as memory, visual recognition, attention, and concentration. Most importantly, we encourage parents to stay involved in their child’s digital experience. Please make sure you are aware of your child’s internet activity and help them develop safe and healthy online habits.

Learning through play, with a little guidance from you, can make a big difference. 🌟
